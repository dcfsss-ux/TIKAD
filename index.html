Cultural Root
ᜄᜒᜌ
[gî-yâ] Filipino · Bisaya
"To Guide" · "A Compass"

Derived from the native Filipino and Bisaya word meaning to navigate, guide, or direct — capturing the core mission of CSU's intelligent 3D campus navigation platform.

Smart Wayfinding 3D Innovation

The Name Behind the Map

ᜄᜒᜌ is more than a map — it is a platform designed to help every student, faculty member, and visitor navigate Caraga State University with ease and confidence.

A Name Rooted in Home

Filipino & Bisaya Heritage

The word GIYA comes from a language familiar to the people of Mindanao, giving the platform a name that feels connected to the community it serves.

A Purpose to Guide

From Meaning to Function

GIYA was named with one simple purpose in mind: to help people find their way around CSU with less confusion and more confidence.

A Digital Campus Companion

Guidance Beyond a Traditional Map

By combining an interactive campus map with accessible building information, GIYA transforms the idea of a simple map into a more helpful campus companion.
